Visit 03 — plantation & maintenance activity

Plantation

Our third CEPL activity moved from observation to action: the team planted a sapling on open ground beside a paved walkway, watered it at its base and documented the site — the “Plantation and Maintenance” activity of our project.

📍 LocationGorksha Nagar / Sneha Nagar, Nashik
🌱 FocusPlantation & maintenance
📅 Date16 October 2026
🧭 GPS20.035036° N, 73.801979° E
Field observation

A sapling was planted in bare soil beside an interlocking brick walkway, with a small white label placed on the soil at its base, then watered. The photographs carry GPS stamps from which the location, date and coordinates were recorded.

Connecting community observations with environmental data

Observations collected during field visits are organised around vegetation, plantation, location and surrounding environmental conditions — so that a photograph taken in the field can be read together with the survey data for the same area.

🌍 From observation to analysis

The Community Hub records the field activity, while Data & Maps organises the corresponding photographs, coordinates and environmental evidence.

  • 🌿 Environment — surrounding conditions noted on site
  • 🌱 Vegetation — species and plantation areas observed
  • 📍 Location — GPS coordinates recorded per visit
  • 📊 Data — survey responses linked to the same area
